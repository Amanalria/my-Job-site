UP Home Guard PET Date Notice 2026

Post Date: August 18, 2026

Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB), has released the PET Date Notice on its official website for the recruitment of UP Home Guard Posts. The DV / PST exam will be conducted from 13 July to 07 August 2026, and the last date to submit the application form was 17 December 2025. Candidates will able to check their DV / PST Admit Card using their Enrollment Number, Registration Number, or Date of Birth by logging into the portal. The direct link has provided below. According to experts, candidates who have correctly answered around 70 to 75 questions should consider starting preparation for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET/PST). It is important to note that a minimum of 25% marks (25 marks) is required to qualify in the written examination; however, a higher score, as mentioned above, will be necessary to secure a place in the merit list.

 How To Check & Download UP Police Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card 2026 
  • Visit the official UP Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) website.
  • Click on the “UP Police Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card 2026” link available on the homepage.
  • Enter your Registration Number/Roll Number and Date of Birth/Password.
  • Fill in the Security Captcha and click on the Login/Submit button.
  • Your DV / PST Admit Card 2026 will appear on the screen.
  • Check all the details, including your DV/PST date, reporting time, venue, and important instructions.
  • Download the admit card and save it for future reference.
  • Take a clear printout of the admit card and carry it along with the required original documents and a valid photo ID proof on the day of Document Verification (DV) and Physical Standard Test (PST).

 UP Police Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card 2026 : Important Question 
  • Question: When will the UP Police Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card 2026 be released?
  • Answer: The UP Police Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card 2026 will be released online before the Document Verification (DV) and Physical Standard Test (PST). Candidates can download it using their login credentials.
  • Question: How can I download the UP Police Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card 2026?
  • Answer: Visit the official UP Police recruitment website, log in using your Registration Number/Roll Number and Date of Birth/Password, and download your admit card.
  • Question: What details are mentioned on the UP Police Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card 2026?
  • Answer: The admit card generally contains the candidate’s name, roll number, registration number, DV/PST date, reporting time, venue, photograph, signature, and important instructions.
  • Question: Is it mandatory to carry the UP Police Home Guard DV / PST Admit Card to the DV/PST venue?
  • Answer: Yes. Candidates must carry a printed copy of the admit card along with a valid original photo ID proof and the required original documents. Entry will not be allowed without these documents.

  • Answer: 18 November 2025.
  • Question: What was the last date to apply for the UP Police Home Guard Online Form 2025?
  • Answer: 17 December 2025.

  • Ans. for General/EBC/EWS candidates : ₹400/- & for SC/ST/Female candidates : ₹300/-.
  • Question: What was the age limit for the UP Police Home Guard Recruitment 2025?
  • Ans. 18 years To 30 years
