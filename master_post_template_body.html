How to Fill Railway RRC NFR Apprentice Online Application 2026
  • Candidates can submit their online applications on the official RRC NFR recruitment portal before the closing date.
  • Carefully read the official advertisement (Advt. No. NFR/NAPS/ACT APP./2026) to verify trade eligibility and division options.
  • Ensure you have all necessary documents handy: Class 10th marksheet, NCVT/SCVT ITI certificate, Aadhaar card, caste certificate (if applicable), passport size photo, and digital signature.
  • Fill out the registration form accurately, upload the scanned images in prescribed format, and verify all entered details before submission.
  • Pay the application fee online through UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, or Internet Banking if required (Female, SC, ST, and PwBD aspirants are exempted).
  • Download and preserve a printed copy of the final application acknowledgment for verification and merit list checking.

Railway RRC NFR Apprentice 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. What is the total number of vacancies in Railway NFR Apprentice 2026?

Ans: A total of 6,777 apprentice training slots are released across various NFR divisions and workshops.

Q. What is the required educational qualification for NFR Apprentice?

Ans: Candidates must have passed Class 10th (Matriculation) with minimum 50% marks from a recognized board and possess an ITI Certificate in the relevant trade from NCVT or SCVT.

Q. Is there any written exam for RRC NFR Apprentice selection?

Ans: No, selection is conducted purely based on the merit list prepared using the average percentage of 10th standard and ITI marks.

Q. Where can I check the official notification and application link?

Ans: You can check the direct online apply link and notification PDF under the Important Links section above on StudyTopper.in or visit nfr.indianrailways.gov.in.
