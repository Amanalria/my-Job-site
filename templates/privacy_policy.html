Privacy Policy

How we protect candidate information and visitor privacy on SarkariResult.com.cm.

1. Data Collection & Usage

SarkariResult.com.cm does not require user registration or personal data collection to access exam notices, result lists, syllabus files, or admit card links. All tools (Age Calculator, Photo Resizer, Resume Builder) process data locally on the user's browser without uploading files to our servers.

2. Cookies and Storage

We use local storage only to remember your interface preferences (such as Dark Mode toggle). No intrusive tracking or advertising beacons are deployed on this platform.

3. External Links

Our website contains direct outbound links to official state and central recruitment gateways. When navigating to third-party domains, please review their respective privacy statements.
