RBI Grade B Officer Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 291 Posts

RBI Grade B Recruitment 2026 : Reserve Bank of India (RBI) invites online applications from eligible Indian citizens for recruitment to 291 Grade B Officer positions in General, DEPR, and DSIM streams. Online registration is open until 15 September 2026.

Important Dates Application Fee
  • Online Application Start: 15/08/2026
  • Online Application Last Date: 15/09/2026
  • Fee Payment Last Date: 15/09/2026
  • Phase 1 Examination Date: October 2026
  • Phase 2 Examination Date: November 2026
  • General / OBC / EWS: ₹850/-
  • SC / ST / PwBD: ₹100/-
  • Payment Mode: Online Net Banking / Debit Card / Credit Card / UPI
Age Limit Criteria & Relaxation (As on 01/08/2026)
  • Minimum Age Limit: 21 Years
  • Maximum Age Limit: 30 Years
  • Age Reference Date: Candidates born between 02/08/1996 and 01/08/2005.
  • Age Relaxation: SC/ST: 5 Years, OBC: 3 Years, PwBD: 10 Years as per RBI rules.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply for RBI Grade B Online Form 2026
  1. Visit the official RBI website (rbi.org.in) and navigate to Opportunities@RBI.
  2. Select Recruitment of Officers in Grade ‘B’ (DR).
  3. Register using active email ID and mobile number.
  4. Fill in personal, academic, and category details accurately.
  5. Upload scanned photograph, signature, thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  6. Submit the examination fee online.
  7. Save and print the final submitted confirmation page.
