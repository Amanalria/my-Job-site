SSC GD Constable Recruitment 2026 : Staff Selection Commission has released the official recruitment notification for 39,481 Constable (General Duty) vacancies across central armed police forces including BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, AR, and SSF. Male and female candidates who passed the 10th matriculation exam can submit their online applications directly through the official portal before the deadline.

Important Dates Application Fee
  • Online Application Start: 15 August 2026
  • Registration Last Date: 25 September 2026 (11:00 PM)
  • Online Fee Payment Last Date: 26 September 2026
  • Application Form Correction Window: 28-30 September 2026
  • Computer Based Examination (CBE): December 2026 / January 2027
  • Admit Card Release: 10 Days Before Exam
  • General / OBC / EWS: Rs. 100/-
  • SC / ST / Ex-Servicemen: Rs. 0/- (Nil)
  • All Category Female Aspirants: Rs. 0/- (Exempted)
  • Payment Mode: Online Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, UPI or SBI Challan
Age Limit & Relaxation Criteria (As on 01/08/2026)
  • Minimum Age Limit: 18 Years
  • Maximum Age Limit: 23 Years (Candidates born between 02/08/2003 and 01/08/2008)
  • OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) Relaxation: 03 Years Upper Age Relaxation
  • SC / ST Candidates Relaxation: 05 Years Upper Age Relaxation
  • Ex-Servicemen (ESM) Relaxation: 03 Years after deduction of military service

Step-by-Step Guide : How to Fill SSC GD Constable Online Form 2026
  1. Visit the official Staff Selection Commission website (ssc.gov.in) and complete your One Time Registration (OTR) profile if not registered previously.
  2. Log in using your registration number and password, then select 'Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces Examination 2026'.
  3. Verify your personal details, select force preferences (BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, AR, SSF) in your preferred order, and choose 3 exam center cities.
  4. Capture your live webcam photograph ensuring adequate lighting with plain background, and upload your signature in JPG format (10 to 20 KB).
  5. Preview all entered information carefully, pay the application fee online through UPI/cards/net banking, and save the final printable application PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q 1. What is the educational qualification for SSC GD Constable 2026?
Ans: Candidates must have passed Class 10th (Matriculation) from any recognized Board in India on or before the closing date.
Q 2. How many total vacancies are announced in SSC GD Constable 2026?
Ans: A total of 39,481 vacancies have been declared across BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles, and SSF.
Q 3. What is the age limit and relaxation for SSC GD Constable?
Ans: The general age limit is 18 to 23 years as on 01/08/2026, with 3 years relaxation for OBC and 5 years relaxation for SC/ST.
Q 4. What is the application fee for female candidates?
Ans: All female candidates and SC/ST/ESM applicants are 100% exempted from paying the application fee.
Q 5. Where can I find official syllabus and notification PDF?
Ans: You can download the official notification PDF and detailed examination syllabus directly from the links table on StudyTopper.in or ssc.gov.in.
