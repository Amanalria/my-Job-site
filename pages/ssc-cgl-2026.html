How to Fill SSC CGL Online Application Form 2026
  1. Visit the official SSC website at ssc.gov.in and register with your mobile number and email ID.
  2. Fill in personal details, educational qualifications, and preferred post in the application form.
  3. Upload passport-size photograph, signature, and required documents in the specified format and file size.
  4. Pay the application fee of ₹100/- online via Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, or UPI (SC/ST/PwD/Female candidates are exempted).
  5. Review all filled details carefully, submit the application, and download the confirmation page for future reference.
  6. Resize your photo and signature to required dimensions using the StudyTopper Tools image resizer at indtool.in.

 

SSC CGL Recruitment 2026 : Selection Process
  • Tier-I : Computer Based Examination (CBE) – Objective Type
  • Tier-II : Computer Based Examination (CBE) – Objective + Descriptive
  • Tier-III : Skill Test / Computer Proficiency Test (where applicable)
  • Document Verification : After Final Merit List

SSC CGL 2026 : Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q1. What is the last date to apply for SSC CGL 2026?
Ans: The online application window closes on 07 September 2026 at ssc.gov.in.
Q2. What is the educational qualification required for SSC CGL 2026?
Ans: Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree from any recognized university or equivalent for most posts.
Q3. What is the age limit for SSC CGL Recruitment 2026?
Ans: The age limit ranges from 18 to 32 years as on 01 January 2027, with category-wise relaxations applicable.
Q4. How much is the application fee for SSC CGL 2026?
Ans: The fee is ₹100/- for General/OBC/EWS candidates. SC/ST/PwD and Female candidates are fully exempted.
Q5. How many stages are there in the SSC CGL 2026 selection process?
Ans: Selection involves Tier-I CBE, Tier-II CBE, Tier-III Skill/Typing Test (for applicable posts), and Document Verification.
Q6. What is the total number of vacancies in SSC CGL 2026?
Ans: SSC CGL 2026 has released 17,838 vacancies across various Group B and Group C posts in Central Government departments.
