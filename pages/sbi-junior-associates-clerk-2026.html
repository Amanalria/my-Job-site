SBI Junior Associates Clerk Recruitment 2026 Apply Online for 9124 Posts

SBI Clerk Recruitment 2026 : State Bank of India SBI has released notification for the recruitment of Junior Associates Customer Support and Sales. Those candidates who are interested in this vacancy can apply online from 11 August to 31 August 2026. Read the notification for recruitment eligibility, post information, selection procedure, age limit, pay scale and all other information.

Important Dates Application Fee
  • Online Application Start: 11/08/2026
  • Online Application Last Date: 31/08/2026
  • Fee Payment Last Date: 31/08/2026
  • Preliminary Exam Date: September / October 2026
  • Main Examination Date: November / December 2026
  • General / OBC / EWS: ₹750/-
  • SC / ST / PwBD / ESM: ₹0/- (Exempted)
  • Payment Mode: Online Net Banking / Debit Card / Credit Card / UPI
Age Limit Criteria & Relaxation (As on 01/04/2026)
  • Minimum Age Limit: 20 Years
  • Maximum Age Limit: 28 Years
  • Age Reference Date: Candidates born between 02/04/1998 and 01/04/2006 (both days inclusive).
  • Age Relaxation: SC/ST: 5 Years, OBC: 3 Years, PwBD: 10 to 15 Years as per SBI recruitment rules.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Fill SBI Clerk Online Form 2026
  1. Visit the official SBI career portal or click the direct application link in the table below.
  2. Select Recruitment of Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales).
  3. Click on Click Here for New Registration and enter basic contact details.
  4. Fill in academic qualifications, exam circle preference, and reservation category accurately.
  5. Upload scanned passport photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  6. Pay application fee through online payment gateway (Cards/UPI/Net Banking).
  7. Download and print the final submitted registration form for future reference.
